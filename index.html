ABOUT CLEARVUE

A clearer approach to everyday eyewear.

ClearVue was created around a simple everyday problem: people who wear eyeglasses often struggle with reduced visibility when rain and water droplets accumulate on their lenses.

Traditional glasses provide vision correction but offer no practical way to remove water while they are being worn. Users often have to stop, remove their glasses, and wipe the lenses manually.

ClearVue explores a different approach by integrating a compact mechanical wiping system directly into the eyewear frame.

The goal is simple: help users maintain clearer visibility while keeping their hands free during rainy and wet conditions.
